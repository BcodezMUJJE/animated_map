ABOUT MOTION ATLAS

SEE THE WORLD MOVE.

Motion Atlas turns geographic data into animated visual stories. Fifty-nine cities. Sixty-one corridors. A decade of movement — trade, migration, transport, connectivity — flowing across the whole planet, day and night.

DATA→MAP→MOTION→STORY

Global Mode arcs across every ocean — including corridors that wrap the antimeridian. East Africa Mode traces the corridors binding Kampala, Nairobi, Kigali, Dar es Salaam, Addis Ababa and Juba. The timeline replays 2015 → 2025. Ask the Map answers pattern-matched commands — a simulated assistant, no external AI.

All figures are simulated demo data for demonstration purposes. Basemap geometry © Natural Earth (public domain). Rendered with a hand-rolled Canvas 2D mercator engine — zero hard dependencies.
